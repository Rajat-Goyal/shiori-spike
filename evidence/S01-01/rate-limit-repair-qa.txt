Residual risk
- The process-wide bucket allows any remote caller to deny owner login for at
  most the 60-second block window after five failures. This is the explicit
  single-owner trade-off that avoids trusting spoofable proxy headers.
- The limiter is in memory, so a process restart clears it and multiple
  replicas would not share failures. Slice 01 is constrained to one replica;
  scaling or stronger abuse resistance requires a shared trusted limiter.
